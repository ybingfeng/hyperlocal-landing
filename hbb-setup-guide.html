Hyperlocal / HBB Setup Guide
For Home-Based Businesses

Get your HBB listed on Hyperlocal

Follow these steps to set up your business, configure your offerings, and start receiving orders — all from your phone.

1
Download the app & sign up

Download Hyperlocal from the App Store (03/05/2026) or Google Play (coming soon!), then create an account using your email, Google, or Apple account.

2
Submit your listing

Tap the My Business tab and fill in your business details — name, category, description, address, and contact number. Once submitted, your listing will be Under Review. We'll notify you once it's approved and live on Hyperlocal.

3
Set up your My Business page
Once your listing is approved, complete the steps below before customers can order from you

When your listing is approved, your status changes to Active and your full My Business dashboard becomes available. This is where everything is managed. There are four things you must complete before your business is ready to take orders.

Requirements before you can take orders:
  • At least 1 product or service added
  • At least 1 open day set within the current month
  • Stripe account connected to receive payments
  • At least one fulfillment method enabled (Pickup or Delivery)

Products & Services REQUIRED

Tap Products & Services to add what you're selling. Each item needs a name and a price. You can add up to 5 photos per item.

  • Variation Groups — if your product comes in different sizes or flavours, add a variation group (e.g. "Size: Small, Large"). Each option can have its own price add-on, or none if the base price applies.
  • Max Quantity Per Time Slot — caps how many units can be ordered in a single time block. Set this to prevent overbooking.
  • Bundles — group 2 or more existing products into a single listing for gift sets or value packs.
Tip: You must add standard products before you can create a bundle from them.

Opening Hours REQUIRED

Tap Opening Hours to set the dates you're available. You must have at least one open day set within the current month.

  • Toggle Open on this day for each date you're available
  • Add Time Blocks within each open day (e.g. 10am–12pm, 2pm–5pm) — customers will pick a slot when ordering. You can set up to 5 blocks per day.
  • Use Weekly Preset to bulk-apply your schedule to the next several weeks at once, so you don't have to set each date manually.
Tip: If no time blocks are set on an open day, customers see you're open but with no specific hours to choose from. Always add at least one time block.

Order Settings REQUIRED

In the Order Settings section on the My Business page, you must enable at least one fulfillment method.

  • Accept Pickup Orders — customers collect their order from your location
  • Accept Delivery Orders — customers request delivery to their address; set your delivery fee (SGD) if you enable this
  • Advance Notice Required — set the minimum number of days customers must order in advance (options range from same day up to 3 weeks)

Tap Save Order Settings once done. You cannot save if neither pickup nor delivery is enabled.

Wallet — Connect Stripe REQUIRED

Tap the Wallet icon on your dashboard to connect your Stripe account. This is required to receive payments from orders. If Stripe isn't fully connected, a red badge will appear on the Wallet icon as a reminder.

  • Tap Connect Stripe to begin onboarding — you'll be guided through Stripe's setup flow
  • If you've started but not finished, the button shows Complete Stripe Setup
  • Once fully connected, you'll see your available balance and payout history here
Note: Listing on Hyperlocal is free. We charge a 5% platform fee per order, capped at $60/month. Once the cap is reached, subsequent orders incur only the payment processing fee.

Other dashboard shortcuts

  • Orders — view and manage incoming orders; confirm, complete, or cancel
  • Messages — respond to customer enquiries before and after orders
  • Edit Listing Details — update your business name, description, photos, address, or contact info at any time
4
Tips for a successful listing
  • Great photos sell products — add clear, well-lit photos for each item; natural lighting works well
  • Write detailed descriptions — mention ingredients, allergens, and what makes your product special
  • Keep your schedule accurate — update your opening hours when you're on leave or during holidays
  • Respond to messages quickly — fast replies build trust and often lead to confirmed orders
  • Ask happy customers for reviews — positive reviews improve your visibility in search
