FAQs for HBBs

Yes, completely. Creating your listing, adding products, managing orders, setting your schedule, and receiving payouts are all free — forever. The only cost is a 1.3% PayNow processing fee charged by Stripe when customers place an order. That fee goes to Stripe, not to us. There is no platform fee, no commission on sales, and no monthly subscription for the mobile app.

You connect your business to Stripe via the Wallet section in the app. Stripe handles all payments — customers pay via PayNow at checkout, and funds are deposited into your Stripe-linked bank account. You can view your available balance and payout history from the Wallet screen anytime. Stripe Connect is required before you can accept orders; if it's not connected, a red badge will appear on the Wallet icon as a reminder.

Hyperlocal is open to any Singapore-based home-based business. Current categories include Bakery, Food & Beverage, Catering, Beauty & Wellness, Handicraft, Fashion, and more. If you're operating a legitimate HBB in Singapore, you're welcome to apply. We review each listing before it goes live to ensure quality for customers.

We typically review new listings within 1–3 business days. You'll receive a push notification once your listing is approved and live. If we need more information, we'll reach out via your contact details. Your listing shows as "Under Review" in the app until it's approved.

Yes. In Order Settings, you can enable pickup, delivery, or both. If you offer delivery, you set your own delivery fee in SGD. You can also set an advance notice period — same-day up to 3 weeks — so customers must order in advance. You cannot save Order Settings unless at least one fulfilment method is enabled.

Each product has a Max Quantity Per Time Slot setting. Once the limit is hit for a slot, it shows as full and customers can't book it. Combined with time blocks on your schedule (e.g. 10am–12pm, 2pm–5pm), you have full control over how many orders you take at any given time. Use the weekly preset in Opening Hours to set your recurring schedule in bulk rather than one day at a time.

We don't require proof of a licence to list. However, you are responsible for ensuring your business complies with relevant Singapore regulations. For example, home-based food businesses should check SFA requirements; for HDB-based businesses, the HDB Home-Based Business Scheme applies. For most craft, beauty, and non-food HBBs there are no specific licensing requirements. When in doubt, check with the relevant authority before listing.

Hyperlocal Pro is an optional subscription ($19.90/mo early bird, rising to $29.90/mo) that unlocks: a full browser-based seller dashboard, broadcast messages to your followers, a loyalty stamp card programme, group buys, and business analytics. You don't need Pro to sell — all the core essentials are free forever. Pro is for sellers who want to grow their customer base, run marketing campaigns, and get deeper insights into their business. There's a 30-day free trial with no card charged upfront.

Broadcasts (available on Pro) let you send a push notification directly to all customers who have saved or previously ordered from your business. You can attach a product or a voucher to the broadcast — perfect for announcing new products, limited runs, or seasonal specials. It's a direct line to your warm customer base, no social media algorithm involved.

Available on Pro, the Loyalty Stamp Card lets you reward repeat customers. You set how many stamps are needed (1–50) and the reward amount in SGD. Customers earn a stamp automatically with each order. When they collect enough stamps, they get a discount reward — encouraging them to keep coming back to your business. You can see live stats on how many customers are participating and how close they are to earning a reward.
